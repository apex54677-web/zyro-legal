Zenomusic Privacy Policy Last Updated: October 14, 2026 1. Introduction Welcome to Zenomusic, a Discord music bot designed to provide music playback and related features within Discord servers. This Privacy Policy explains what information Zenomusic may collect, how that information is used, and how it is handled when you interact with the bot. By using Zenomusic, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 2. Information We May Collect Zenomusic may process the following information when necessary to provide its features: - Discord User ID: Used to identify users who interact with bot commands. - Discord Server ID: Used to identify servers where the bot operates. - Voice Channel ID: Used to connect the bot to the requested voice channel. - Text Channel ID: Used to send music playback updates and command responses. - Music Requests: Search queries and YouTube URLs submitted through music commands. - Playback Information: Current tracks, queue entries, playback settings, volume, and selected audio filters. - Technical Information: Error messages and diagnostic information generated when troubleshooting technical problems. The information processed depends on the features you use and the bot's current configuration. 3. How We Use Information Information may be used to: - Execute music commands and provide playback functionality. - Search for and play supported YouTube content. - Maintain music queues and playback settings. - Connect to the appropriate Discord voice and text channels. - Respond to user requests and troubleshoot errors. - Maintain the security, reliability, and performance of the bot. Zenomusic does not need your Discord password to provide its normal bot functionality. 4. Music Services and Third Parties Zenomusic may interact with Discord and YouTube to provide its services. When you request music, your search query or requested URL may be processed by YouTube-related tools or services used by the bot. Your use of Discord and YouTube is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service. Zenomusic is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord or YouTube unless explicitly stated otherwise. 5. Data Storage and Retention Some information may be processed temporarily in memory while the bot is operating, including music queues, playback information, and server settings. Technical logs or other information may be retained when necessary for troubleshooting, security, or maintenance. We do not claim that all information is automatically deleted immediately after a command or when the bot stops. Actual retention depends on the bot's configuration and hosting environment. 6. Data Sharing We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord, YouTube-related services, or hosting providers when necessary to operate the bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service. We aim to limit information processing to what is reasonably necessary for the bot's operation. 7. Data Security Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by Zenomusic. Users should never share their Discord passwords, authentication tokens, or other account credentials with the bot or its operators. 8. Your Choices You can stop interacting with Zenomusic at any time and can remove the bot from a Discord server if you have the necessary permissions. If you have questions about information associated with your use of Zenomusic or wish to request deletion of retained information, you can contact the bot owner through the official support channel, if one is available. 9.
